UPSC Exam Calendar 2026: Complete Schedule & Important Dates

UPSC Updated: Feb 27, 2026 5 min read

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts some of the most prestigious examinations in India, including the Civil Services Examination (CSE), National Defence Academy (NDA), Combined Defence Services (CDS), and many more. This comprehensive guide covers all UPSC exams scheduled for 2026.

Quick Update

UPSC has released the official examination calendar for 2026. All dates mentioned below are as per the official notification. Subscribe to IndiaGovWatch for instant updates on any changes.

UPSC Civil Services Examination 2026

The Civil Services Examination is conducted for recruitment to various services including IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS, and other Group A & B services of the Government of India.

Event Date
Notification Release February 14, 2026
Application Start Date February 14, 2026
Application Last Date March 6, 2026
Prelims Exam Date May 25, 2026
Mains Exam Date September 2026 (Tentative)
Interview January-April 2027

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 21-32 years (relaxation for reserved categories)
  • Education: Bachelor's degree from a recognized university
  • Attempts: General - 6, OBC - 9, SC/ST - Unlimited (up to age limit)
  • Nationality: Indian citizen

NDA & Naval Academy Examination 2026

The National Defence Academy (NDA) examination is conducted twice a year for admission to Army, Navy, and Air Force wings of NDA and Naval Academy.

Exam Notification Exam Date
NDA (I) 2026 December 2025 April 13, 2026
NDA (II) 2026 May 2026 September 6, 2026

CDS Examination 2026

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) examination is for recruitment to Indian Military Academy, Officers Training Academy, Indian Naval Academy, and Indian Air Force Academy.

Exam Notification Exam Date
CDS (I) 2026 October 2025 April 5, 2026
CDS (II) 2026 May 2026 September 13, 2026

Other UPSC Examinations 2026

CAPF (AC) Examination

Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination for BSF, CRPF, ITBP, CISF, and SSB.

  • Notification: April 2026
  • Exam Date: August 2, 2026

Indian Engineering Services (IES/ESE)

Engineering Services Examination for recruitment to technical services.

  • Notification: September 2025
  • Prelims: February 8, 2026
  • Mains: June 28, 2026

Indian Forest Service (IFS)

Conducted along with Civil Services for recruitment to Indian Forest Service.

  • Prelims: May 25, 2026 (along with CSE Prelims)
  • Mains: November 2026
